Campaign Architecture

Save the Farm: 6-Level Progression

The interactive training journey spans six core levels, bridging foundational farm diagnostics with cooperative land-linking. As participants progress, they transform Farmer X from a skeptical lone operator into a staunch community ally, culminating in independent micro-farm leases.

Level 1

The Intake & Diagnostic Gate

Narrative: Meet Farmer X on his Linn County homestead after a crippling tractor accident. With foreclosure looming and single-commodity markets collapsed, you complete initial demographic intake and basic ledger audits to diagnose the financial bleeding.

Level 2

Extension Connection & Cash Flow Extension

Narrative: You link Farmer X with the OSU Extension service to introduce quick-turn greens and specialty mushroom crops. This buys valuable time, pushing the bank foreclosure notice back to next year's harvest line.

Level 3

Soil Armor & Climate Resilience

Narrative: To combat summer heat waves and winter downpours without preaching climate jargon, you apply biochar sourced directly from veteran fire scar remediation teams in regional watersheds, locking moisture and protecting root systems.

Level 4

The Alliance Shift & Labor Sharing

Narrative: Farmer X—initially deeply skeptical of outsiders and younger trainees—realizes your modern techniques and consistent physical support make you a trusted ally. Together, you establish shared labor routines to manage daily operations.

Level 5

Harvest Scaling & Food Security Distribution

Narrative: Production hits commercial volume. You coordinate local market drop-offs and allocate dedicated yield shares to community food banks, satisfying vital grant metrics and building deep local goodwill.

Level 6

The Cooperative Lease Vanguard

Narrative: With the bank boss permanently defeated and yields secured, Farmer X champions your group. You finalize formal share-crop agreements and unlock individual micro-farm leases on the restored acreage, achieving true agricultural independence.
