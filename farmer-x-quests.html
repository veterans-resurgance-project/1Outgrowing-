Chapter One: The Homestead

The game opens on the porch of Farmer X’s homestead just outside Lebanon, Oregon. A rusted John Deere sits idle in the distance, its front loader frozen mid-lift. A weathered foreclosure notice from the bank is pinned to the screen door, fluttering in the early autumn wind.

Farmer X sits nursing a cracked rib and a heavily braced arm, staring at empty ledger books. For twenty years, he did everything alone until a PTO shaft caught his jacket. Now, a lost season has wiped out cash flow, and his traditional wholesale buyer dropped his contract.

As the incoming trainee, you must step up and open the first ledger to learn how the business actually survives.

Chapter 1 (Quest 1): The Crop Diversification Trap

The Dilemma: Farmer X relied 100% on a single commodity crop that just crashed in market value.

Game Challenge: Select the strategy that protects a small farm from total market collapse.

Survival & Resilience Sidetrack: The Timber Camp

Setback: Shortcuts or bad strategies collapse your enterprise. Farmer X sends you out to the timber lot: "If you're going to survive out here when systems fail, learn how nature adapts."

Foraging Lesson: You live in a canvas tent for a week, learning to forage wild chanterelles and oyster mushrooms while avoiding toxic lookalikes like *Galerina marginata*. You gain resource awareness before returning to the ledger.

Chapter 1 (Quest 2): The Physical Audit

The Dilemma: With Farmer X's mobility limited, tasks that took one hour now take a full day.

Game Challenge: Identify how to handle core farm tasks safely without risking injury.

Chapter 1 (Quest 3): The Fixed-Cost Reality Check

The Dilemma: Property taxes and equipment loans are due in 90 days with zero revenue coming in.

Game Challenge: Calculate immediate cash burn rate and determine acreage allocation.

Chapter 1 (Quest 4): Soil Baseline Calibration

The Dilemma: Before planting, you must set baseline nutrient parameters for the valley floor.

Game Challenge: Identify the correct target soil pH baseline discussed on the homestead porch.

Level 2 Security Code Gate

You have successfully answered all four diagnostic quests! To unlock the heavy iron doors leading into Level 2: The Tool Shed & Soil Management, enter the 4-digit master clearance code from your field manual.

Hint: The baseline soil target numbers from Farmer X's notes start with this sequence.

Chapter Two: Level 2 Tool Shed Unlocked!

The heavy iron gate clicks open, releasing the sharp scent of linseed oil and iron. Farmer X tosses you a tarnished brass soil core sampler.

"You passed the gate, kid. Now comes the real field work," Farmer X grunts, sliding over the soil testing kit. You are officially ready to advance deeper into production management.
