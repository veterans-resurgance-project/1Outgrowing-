Interactive Journey Simulator

From Ground Zero to Agricultural Apprentice

Complete real-world milestones, build essential community connections, and earn XP as you forge your path from urban or transitional hardships directly into Linn County’s agricultural training ecosystem.

📚 Stage 1: Library Resources & Community Navigation

Location: Public Library Branch

Your journey begins at the local public library terminal. Complete these foundational tasks to establish your digital footprint, secure paperwork, and earn XP.

🤝 Stage 2: Day Center & Advocacy Intake

Location: Community Day Center

Once core library benchmarks are cleared, the journey transitions directly into the local Day Center Program and dedicated Community Advocate intake.

🚜 Next Location: Transitional Resource & Employment Hub

With your core paperwork organized and your support network established at the day center, your pathway leads directly to the regional Transitional Resource & Employment Hub.

🌱 Vocational Intake
Connect with workforce development specialists to review job training opportunities, such as agricultural apprenticeships or commercial driver's license prep programs.
🛡️ Supportive Services Coordination
Access on-site assistance for temporary housing placements, hygiene resource vouchers, and clothing closets tailored for upcoming interviews or field work.
