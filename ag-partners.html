Co-Beneficial Model & Pathways

Farmer & Nursery Partnerships & Specialized Tracks

Bridging Willamette Valley agricultural producers with structured youth mentorship, community food security initiatives, and rigorous vocational tracks.

Host Farm & Nursery MOUs

Our program establishes formal Memorandums of Understanding (MOUs) with vetted Willamette Valley commercial farms, crop producers, and wholesale nurseries. We create a structured, legally sound framework where growers receive reliable, stipend-supported participant labor during critical seasonal windows, while youth apprentices gain generational mentorship and tangible trade experience.

What We Bring to Partners

Reliable participant labor forces, structured behavioral and safety oversight, and minimized administrative friction through our nonprofit framework.

Nursery & Field Scope

Covers both open-field agricultural production and controlled-environment wholesale nursery operations (propagation, potting, and stock management).

Community Service & Food Security Integration

Service to community is core to our mission. A dedicated percentage of all crop yields and apiary harvest is ring-fenced for direct community distribution.

  • Fresh Produce & Honey Boxes: Assembling and distributing seasonal vegetable boxes and raw honey jars directly to local veterans, food banks, and vulnerable families in Linn County.
  • Civic Responsibility: Instilling a deep sense of mutual aid in our youth participants by connecting their daily fieldwork directly to local food sovereignty and resilience.

Apiary & Honey Bee Management Track

An intensive vocational module focusing on commercial beekeeping and ecological support.

Hive Husbandry

Training on colony health inspections, pest management, queen introduction, and seasonal hive maintenance.

Harvest & Pollination

Executing honey extraction, bottling, comb care, and deploying hives for farm crop pollination enhancement.

Advanced, Safety & Inclusive Vocational Tracks

Machinery & Mechanics

Hands-on training in tractor operation, small engine repair, irrigation pump maintenance, and farm equipment fabrication.

Food Safety & Compliance

Rigorous instruction on GAP (Good Agricultural Practices), post-harvest hygiene, temperature logs, and facility sanitization.

Performance Excellence

Accelerated leadership pipelines for hard-working participants, preparing them for crew foreman and farm manager roles.

Adaptive Accessibility

Customized agricultural workflows, raised planting beds, ergonomic tools, and modified tasks tailored for individuals with disabilities.
