1. The Mutual Growth Network Model

Cross-Generational Mentorship & Exchange

The Mutual Growth Network functions as the human infrastructure of both Outgrowing-Ag and the Veterans Resurgence Project (VRP). Rather than a unidirectional service model, it builds a reciprocal loop between seasoned legacy homesteaders, participating veterans, and incoming trainees.

  • Legacy Knowledge Transfer: Capturing decades of localized agronomic wisdom, water management, and hand-tool craft from senior land stewards.
  • Peer-Led Leadership: Empowering veterans transitioning out of service to step into crew leadership, logistics management, and field oversight roles.
  • Community Resilience: Establishing sustainable local food networks and mutual aid loops across rural Willamette Valley and Linn County corridors.

2. Winter Term Training Module Matrix

Module / Focus Track Core Curriculum & Skills Covered Operational Application
Module 1: Ag & Soil Restoration Compost management, orchard pruning, drip irrigation repair, and basic micro-farm soil health analysis. Weekend field deployments on senior and legacy homestead sites.
Module 2: Timber & Watershed Safety Heavy PPE protocols, chainsaw safety & maintenance, brush clearing, and post-wildfire hazard awareness. VRP field operations, forest floor remediation, and watershed cleanup.
Module 3: Aerial Tech & Mapping Drone flight operations, Part 107 ground school basics, and aerial mapping for watershed monitoring. Remote site surveying, erosion tracking, and project documentation.
Module 4: Nursery Propagation Native plant propagation, root stock management, seed collection, and greenhouse tray mechanics. Long-term reforestation stock production and site revegetation.

3. Detailed Curriculum Progression

Phase 1

Foundational Readiness & Safety Standards

OSHA 10-Hour Agricultural Safety Hazard identification, equipment safety protocols, and field mitigation guidelines.
First Aid & CPR Certification Essential emergency response training tailored for remote and rural farm environments.
Commercial Driver's License (CDL) Prep Knowledge testing support for airbrakes and passenger/transport endorsements.
Phase 2

Specialized Crop Mastery & Field Operations

Soil Health & Regenerative Inputs Organic soil amendment management, cover cropping, and biological soil web mechanics.
Irrigation & Water Infrastructure Drip line management, moisture tracking, and efficient Willamette Valley water distribution.
Apiary & Pollinator Management Integrated hive care, seasonal maintenance, and supporting local farm ecosystem health.
Phase 3

Enterprise Launch & Land Integration

Micro-Farm Plot Management Hands-on execution on host-farm plots under the Shared Harvest cooperative network.
Direct Market Logistics & Compliance Harvest handling, food safety compliance, and regional distribution channels.
