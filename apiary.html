Specialized Track

Apiary & Pollinator Management

A specialized vocational module focused on honeybee colony health, hive maintenance, seasonal inspections, and supporting vital agricultural pollination across the Willamette Valley.

1. Hive Architecture & Equipment Maintenance

Hands-on construction, assembly, and maintenance of Langstroth hives, supers, frames, and protective gear. Understanding equipment hygiene and seasonal preparation.

2. Colony Inspection & Health Monitoring

Learning safe hive opening protocols, identifying queen health and brood patterns, managing swarm behaviors, and monitoring for pests and diseases using integrated management practices.

3. Honey Extraction & Pollination Integration

Best practices for honey harvesting, uncapping, extraction, bottling, and integrating apiary placements with neighboring host-farm crops to maximize regional pollination impact.
