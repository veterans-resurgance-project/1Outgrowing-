Agreement Template 03

Community Food Security & Distribution Pact

Governs the harvest donation pipeline connecting AFTI training yields directly with Linn County food pantries and community distribution channels.

  • Quality & Safety Standards: Guarantees adherence to food safety and cold-chain handling best practices during post-harvest sorting.
  • Volume & Cadence: Sets recurring delivery schedules or on-call surplus notification protocols during peak harvest cycles.
  • Community Impact Tracking: Logs poundage distributed to measure direct contributions toward mitigating local food insecurity.
