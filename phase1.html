Phase 1 Curriculum

Orientation & Soil Health

The foundational entry point for all participants. This module establishes core agricultural safety standards, regional Willamette Valley ecology, and baseline soil science.

1. Program Orientation & Safety Standards

Participants receive thorough orientation regarding host-farm protocols, tool handling safety, emergency preparedness, and expectations for working cooperatively within Linn County agricultural extension networks.

2. Willamette Valley Agroecosystems

An introduction to local climate patterns, watershed preservation principles, native plant communities, and the unique environmental characteristics that make the Willamette Valley vital for sustainable farming.

3. Baseline Soil Biology & Chemistry

Hands-on learning focused on soil structure, organic matter composition, pH testing, microbial activity, and cover cropping strategies designed to enhance long-term fertility without synthetic over-reliance.
