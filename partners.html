For Farmers & Advisory Partners

Building specialized, trusted contract teams for Willamette Valley agriculture. Act as advisory partners to help shape our training protocols and cultivate a private, reliable pipeline of labor custom-trained to your operation's exact standards.

1. Co-Designed SOP Training & Custom Pipelines

As an advisory partner, you inform the core training curriculum. By integrating your specific standard operating procedures (SOPs) and equipment protocols into our modules, you create a private, dedicated pipeline of labor pre-trained and ready to deploy directly to your enterprise.

2. Trusted Specialized Contract Teams

Move beyond the traditional revolving door of untrained seasonal labor. Our vetted active cohorts deploy as professional, cohesive contractor teams built on mutual accountability, discipline, and absolute respect for your land, crops, and infrastructure.

3. Scalable Surge Capacity via Prep Waitlist

Backed by our preparatory waitlist, we maintain deep bench strength to help operations scale up seamlessly during peak harvest surges without sacrificing quality, safety, or operational continuity.
