Partnership & Advisory Networks

For Farmers & Landowners

Revitalize under-utilized Willamette Valley acreage, secure reliable professional teams, and co-design standardized operating procedures that align directly with your operation's exact production requirements.

1. Co-Designed SOP Training & Custom Labor Pipelines

Traditional seasonal hiring forces growers to continually retrain revolving, unvetted labor. Through our advisory partnership framework, you integrate your specific standard operating procedures (SOPs), equipment protocols, and quality benchmarks directly into our curriculum. This establishes a private, dedicated pipeline of pre-trained workers ready to hit your fields with immediate competence.

2. Vetted Professional Teams & Accountability

AFTI cohorts undergo rigorous screening, practical task trials, and leadership tracking in fields like safety compliance and equipment management. When our teams deploy to your farm, they operate as disciplined, accountable units committed to protecting your capital assets, infrastructure, and soil health.

3. Acreage Revitalization & Shared Harvest Integration

For landowners managing dormant or secondary acreage, our network matches vetted micro-farmers and incubator participants to cultivate plots productively. This reduces liability, keeps land tax-exempt or active under agricultural classifications, and generates supplemental cooperative yield streams.

Standardized Agreement Frameworks & MOUs

We maintain transparent, structured legal agreements designed to protect landowner liability while establishing clear operational boundaries for training and access:

Agreement Template 01

Host-Farm Land & Training Access MOU

Defines the parameters for participating agricultural landowners providing acreage or infrastructure access for AFTI trainee development.

  • Scope of Access: Grants designated training access to specific acreage, hoop houses, or utility infrastructure without transferring land title.
  • Safety & Compliance: Commits both parties to mutual adherence to OSHA agricultural safety standards and AFTI supervision protocols.
  • Liability & Insurance: Establishes clear liability distribution, ensuring general liability coverage for vocational trainees on-site.
  • Term & Review: Operates on a seasonal or annual renewal cycle with a 30-day amicable termination clause for either party.
Mutual Growth Framework

How Our Training & Farms Support Each Other

Our vocational program and regional farm network operate as a single, supportive ecosystem. What our trainees learn in the classroom is shaped directly by what our partners need in the field.

What AFTI Brings to Your Land

Reliable, energetic, and disciplined teams trained in core safety and task execution. For smaller homesteads and senior growers, we also help bridge the digital divide—assisting with direct-to-consumer tech, record-keeping, and translating extension service guidance into active field help.

What Farms Bring Back to AFTI

Authentic living classrooms where our participants gain real-world experience. Most importantly, experienced growers pass down invaluable generational wisdom, crop intuition, and regional resilience strategies to our cohorts.
