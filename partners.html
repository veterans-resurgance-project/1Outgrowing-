Partnership Framework

For Farmers & Advisory Networks

Collaborating with Willamette Valley growers, commercial nurseries, and agricultural extension advisors to provide land access, apprentice placement, and mutual operational support.

1. Host-Farm & Nursery MOUs

We establish clear Memorandums of Understanding (MOUs) with regional agricultural operators. These agreements define safe training boundaries, equipment sharing, liability protections, and structured apprentice support.

2. Skilled Labor & Apprentice Integration

Participating farmers gain reliable, motivated agricultural trainees who have completed foundational orientation in soil health, equipment safety, and field crop management.

3. Community Food Security Logistics

Our network coordinates surplus distribution and localized harvest yields directly with Linn County food banks, pantries, and mutual aid networks, ensuring sustainable local impact.
