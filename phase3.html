Phase 3 Curriculum

Enterprise Leadership & Distribution

The culminating phase of vocational training. Participants step into leadership roles, managing harvest logistics, farm business planning, and regional food security distribution networks.

1. Advanced Farm Leadership & Crew Management

Developing supervisory capabilities, leading team safety meetings, delegating daily field tasks, and mentoring incoming Phase 1 and Phase 2 participants across host-farm locations.

2. Harvest Logistics & Post-Harvest Handling

Mastering optimal harvest timing, cold chain storage protocols, washing, grading, and packaging standards required for commercial and community-grade produce.

3. Food Security Networks & Market Distribution

Coordinating supply chains with regional food banks, community pantries, and farmers' markets to ensure sustainable local food distribution throughout Linn County and the Willamette Valley.
