Partnership Framework // OSU Extension & Ag

Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) Template

Standardized partnership agreement governing site identification, agronomic guidance, and community land stewardship coordination between the Extension service, legacy homesteaders, and Outgrowing-Ag crews.

1. Collaborative Scope & Responsibilities

Intent & Purpose

This Memorandum of Understanding establishes the working framework between program administrators, participating regional extension agents, and legacy homestead partners. The objective is to pair disciplined outgrowing-ag labor crews with agricultural properties requiring restorative care, soil management, and infrastructure support.

  • Site Selection: Collaborative identification of high-need micro-farms and senior homesteads via local extension channels.
  • Technical Guidance: Integration of extension-backed agronomic standards, crop rotation guidance, and resource management protocols.
  • Labor Deployment: Execution of weekend work blocks governed by strict safety rules and pre-approved task checklists.

2. Partner Commitment Matrix

Entity / Stakeholder Core Responsibilities Compliance & Risk Standard
Extension Partner Advises on regional agronomic best practices, soil health metrics, and candidate farm suitability. Adherence to non-regulatory educational guidance models.
Legacy Homestead Owner Provides property access, mentorship exchange, and identifies priority restoration zones. Display of mandatory Oregon Agritourism statutory warning signs (ORS 30.673).
Outgrowing-Ag Program Deploys structured weekend labor crews, manages tool logistics, and enforces safety SOPs. Enforcement of signed participant liability waivers and CGL insurance coverage.
