Governance & Collaboration

MOUs & Partner Agreement Frameworks

Review our official partnership frameworks, host-farm protocols, and collaborative agreements designed to protect participants and ensure smooth agricultural operations across Linn County.

Agreement Template 01

Host-Farm Land & Training Access MOU

Defines the parameters for participating agricultural landowners providing acreage or infrastructure access for AFTI trainee development.

  • Scope of Access: Grants designated training access to specific acreage, hoop houses, or utility infrastructure without transferring land title.
  • Safety & Compliance: Commits both parties to mutual adherence to OSHA agricultural safety standards and AFTI supervision protocols.
  • Liability & Insurance: Establishes clear liability distribution, ensuring general liability coverage for vocational trainees on-site.
  • Term & Review: Operates on a seasonal or annual renewal cycle with a 30-day amicable termination clause for either party.
Agreement Template 02

Commercial Nursery & Seedling Supply Agreement

Outlines wholesale or subsidized material support for seeds, starts, and propagation supplies utilized in Phase 2 cultivation tracks.

  • Material Allocation: Specifies types, quantities, and delivery schedules for propagation plugs, rootstock, or soil amendments.
  • Educational Support: Partner nurseries occasionally provide guest mentorship or technical workshops on greenhouse management.
  • Reporting & Tracking: Aligns with AFTI grant class-tracking codes to ensure transparent accounting of donated or discounted inputs.
Agreement Template 03

Community Food Security & Distribution Pact

Governs the harvest donation pipeline connecting AFTI training yields directly with Linn County food pantries and community distribution channels.

  • Quality & Safety Standards: Guarantees adherence to food safety and cold-chain handling best practices during post-harvest sorting.
  • Volume & Cadence: Sets recurring delivery schedules or on-call surplus notification protocols during peak harvest cycles.
  • Community Impact Tracking: Logs poundage distributed to measure direct contributions toward mitigating local food insecurity.
