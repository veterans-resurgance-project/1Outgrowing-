Willamette Valley Agricultural Initiative

Cultivating the Future of Agriculture Through Specialized Teams & Land Access

The Agricultural Future & Training Initiative (AFTI) bridges the critical divide between under-utilized rural land and the next generation of agricultural professionals. By combining rigorous vocational training, custom advisory SOP alignment, and cooperative land-linking across Linn County, we create private contractor teams that deliver mutual economic revitalization and long-term food security.

🌱 For Aspiring Growers

Step into active cohorts or preparatory waitlists, build elite vocational skills, and access land without ownership barriers.

🚜 For Farmers & Landowners

Deploy trusted contractor teams trained in your exact SOPs, unlock secondary income, and keep idle acreage productive.

🤝 For the Community

Protect rural agricultural heritage while allocating fresh harvest yields directly to local food security initiatives.
