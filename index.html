Willamette Valley Agricultural Initiative

Cultivating the Future of Agriculture Through Specialized Teams & Land Access

The Agricultural Future & Training Initiative (AFTI) bridges the critical divide between under-utilized rural land and the next generation of agricultural professionals. By combining rigorous vocational training, custom advisory SOP alignment, and cooperative land-linking across Linn County, we create private trained teams that deliver mutual economic revitalization and food security.

🌱 For Aspiring Growers

Step into active cohorts, build elite vocational skills, and access land without ownership barriers.

🚜 For Landowners

Deploy trained teams in your exact SOPs, unlock secondary income, and keep idle acreage productive.

📈 Regional Economic Stimulus & Land Stewardship

Beyond individual farm training, AFTI acts as a catalyst for local economic vitality. By revitalizing under-utilized acreage and matching dedicated apprentices with retiring landowners, we keep agricultural investments local, preserve active open space, and build generational financial literacy.
