Willamette Valley Agricultural Initiative

Cultivating the Future of Agriculture Through Specialized Teams & Land Access

The Agricultural Future & Training Initiative (AFTI) bridges the critical divide between under-utilized rural land and the next generation of agricultural professionals. By combining rigorous vocational training, custom advisory SOP alignment, and cooperative land-linking across Linn County, we create private trained teams that deliver mutual economic revitalization and long-term food security.
