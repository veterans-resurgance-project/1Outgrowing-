Chapter One: The Homestead

The game opens on the porch of Farmer X’s homestead just outside Lebanon, Oregon. A rusted John Deere sits idle in the distance, its front loader frozen mid-lift. A weathered foreclosure notice from the bank is pinned to the screen door, fluttering in the early autumn wind.

Farmer X sits nursing a cracked rib and a heavily braced arm, staring at empty ledger books. For twenty years, he did everything alone—tilling, planting, harvesting, and hauling—until a PTO shaft caught his jacket and changed everything in a split second. Now, a lost season has wiped out cash flow, and his traditional wholesale buyer dropped his contract.

As the incoming AFTI trainee, you must step up and open the first ledger. To save this farm, you cannot just do physical labor; you have to learn how the business actually survives.

Level 1 (Part 1): The Crop Diversification Trap

The Dilemma: Farmer X relied 100% on a single commodity crop that just crashed in market value.

Game Challenge: Select the strategy that protects a small farm from total market collapse.

Consulting the Extension Agent

Extension Advisor: "Chasing single-commodity booms in the valley is a gambler's game. If the market dips or pests hit that one crop, you're wiped out. Take a look at diversified farm models before touching the soil again."

Level 1 (Part 2): The Physical Audit

The Dilemma: With Farmer X's mobility limited, tasks that took one hour now take a full day, leading to missed windows.

Game Challenge: Identify which core farm tasks can be safely delegated versus what requires specialized machinery.

Safety & Labor Warning

Safety Inspector: "Trying to muscle machinery with an injury is how people end up permanently disabled or dead out here. Respect the safety boundaries and utilize community work-sharing networks."

Level 1 (Part 3): The Fixed-Cost Reality Check

The Dilemma: Property taxes and equipment loans are due in 90 days, but zero revenue is coming in.

Game Challenge: Calculate immediate cash burn rate and determine acreage allocation for debt service.
