The Hub-and-Spoke Model

Industry-Driven Agricultural Education

Operating from our Linn County base, this initiative connects agricultural extension resources with private farm and nursery partners. By incorporating a grower-led Advisory Board, our curriculum directly mirrors the exact skills local producers need in their workforce—from native plant propagation to greenhouse management and commercial field operations.

On-Site Housing Integration

Partnering with regional farms and nurseries offering board and housing as part of apprentice compensation packages, solving housing and employment hurdles simultaneously.

Extension Partnerships

Leveraging university extension networks and agricultural experts to secure USDA, beginning farmer, and workforce development funding streams.
