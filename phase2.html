Phase 2 Curriculum

Active Cultivation & Crop Management

The core operational phase. Participants transition from theory to hands-on field production, mastering greenhouse propagation, irrigation systems, and active crop care.

1. Greenhouse Propagation & Seeding

Hands-on training in seed-starting techniques, transplanting schedules, climate control in hoop houses, and managing seedling health prior to field integration.

2. Seasonal Crop Rotation & Field Care

Executing field bed preparation, direct sowing, weed management strategies, and multi-season crop rotation plans tailored to Willamette Valley weather patterns.

3. Irrigation Infrastructure & Pest Management

Installation and maintenance of drip and sprinkler irrigation lines. Introduction to integrated pest management (IPM), beneficial insect habitat creation, and biological controls.
